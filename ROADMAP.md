# Future audio tracks

Investigate optional alternate dubs from Internet Archive in a future update. No alternate audio is downloaded or enabled by this release.

Check each item's actual audio availability and permitted use; hosting alone does not establish permission. Match the exact film/episode cut, runtime, frame rate, and edit points. Blu-ray audio may have a different initial offset or gradual drift, and alternate cuts can require multiple synchronization points. Prototype with a permitted test pair before adding discovery or automatic synchronization. Keep track selection, offset adjustment, and a way to restore the original audio in the playback menu. Confirm that the Steam Link can demux and decode the external track while hardware video and the bounded disk buffer remain stable.
